Yours, by design

Your numbers stay with you.

No bank connection. No account. Just the numbers you choose to use. Six languages, and your own currency symbol.

  • No bank connection.

    Start with what you have and a usual month, not a financial login.

  • No account.

    Open it and answer two questions.

  • Your records stay on your phone.

    Locked with strong encryption, and the key stays in your iPhone. Unless you back them up to a file of your own.

Questions

What people ask.

Is my money safe?

Runway never sees your bank. You type your numbers; they stay on your iPhone, locked with strong encryption.

Does it work in my country?

Six languages, and your own currency.

What if I get a new phone?

Your iPhone's backup doesn't include Runway. With Runway Pro you can back up to a file you keep and restore it on the new phone.

Why not a spreadsheet?

"I worked it out once, then I had to keep working it out." Runway keeps the answer current in about 30 seconds a week.

Is it really free?

Yes: your runway (how long your money lasts), weekly check-ups, month stories, goals and one what-if are free. Runway Pro is one payment, no subscription.
