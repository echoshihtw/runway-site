What people ask.

How does Financial Runway work out how long my money lasts?

You type what you have, what you owe and what you usually spend. Runway takes the money you can use, divides it by what goes out each month, and shows the months it lasts and the month it reaches.

Do I need to link my bank?

No. There is no account to make and no bank login. You type your balances yourself, and nothing connects to your bank.

Where do my records go?

They stay on your iPhone, in an encrypted database, and are never uploaded. Runway sends anonymous usage counts that never include amounts, balances, names or notes.

Is it free?

Yes. Financial Runway is free on iPhone.

Does it count money coming in?

The main number leaves money coming in out, so it shows how long you would be fine if it stopped. Add your pay, a pension or pocket money, and a second line shows what it adds.

What is a check-up?

Once a week you type today's balances. Runway compares them with what it expected and asks only about the differences, so the number stays true without writing down every spend.
