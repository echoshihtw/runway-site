Common questions

How do I restore Runway Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro. Open Runway, tap Settings on the home screen, then tap Restore purchase. When it works, the Runway Pro row says Yours. If there is nothing to restore, Runway says “No previous purchase found.”

I redeemed a code but Pro is still locked.

Close Runway completely and open it again, then tap Restore purchase in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, in an encrypted database. Runway has no account and no cloud sync. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I erase all my data?

Tap Settings on the home screen, then Erase everything on this phone at the bottom. After you confirm, every number on your iPhone is erased and Runway starts again from the beginning. Runway Pro stays unlocked.

If I delete the app, can I get my data back?

Only from a backup you saved first. Deleting Runway permanently removes its encrypted data from your iPhone, and we never have a copy. If you bought Runway Pro, Back up in Settings saves a copy with a password, and Restore a backup in Settings brings it back. Otherwise, keep the app installed if you want to keep your records.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why does my runway show a certain number of months?

Runway starts from the money you have, minus what you owe. It takes away your everyday spending day by day, and each regular bill on the day it is due, until the money runs out. Pay you expect is left out of the main number, so it shows how long your money lasts if nothing more came in. Your pay shows on a second line, such as “With your pay: 14 months”. At first Runway uses what you said you spend. It gets more exact after your first month, as check-ups measure what you really spend.
